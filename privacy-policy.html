Light the Grid – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Light the Grid ("the game", "we") handles. The game does not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, daily streak, achievements and sound setting are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where allowed);
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later by reinstalling the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
